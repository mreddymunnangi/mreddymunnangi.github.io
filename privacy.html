Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What this app is

YT Shorts Bot is a personal automation tool that uploads short videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

The app asks for one Google permission, youtube.upload. It uses that permission only to upload videos the app has generated to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscribers or account data.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and server, and is used only to upload videos. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party connections page.
